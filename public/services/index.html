Commercial Cleaning

Recurring cleaning for medical, professional, and commercial office buildings. The useful work is the work that fits the building: common areas, offices, restrooms, breakrooms, hard floors, high-touch surfaces, trash and recycling, and other priorities can all belong in the scope conversation.

Planned floor maintenance

Floor Care

Premier handles any floor care need. Commercial flooring carries the wear of the building's traffic, so floor care is treated as planned maintenance and high-use floors do not have to become a visible problem before they receive attention.

The right plan depends on the flooring, traffic, use of the space, and the standard the facility needs to maintain. Specific techniques and timing come from that conversation.

Defined by the facility: The method and timing are shaped by the facility rather than a universal promise.
