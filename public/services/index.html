Planned floor maintenance

Floor Care

Commercial flooring carries the daily wear of the facility. Premier treats floor care as planned maintenance, so high-use floors do not have to become a visible problem before they receive attention.

The right plan depends on the flooring, traffic, use of the space, and the standard the facility needs to maintain. Specific techniques and timing come from that conversation.

Defined by the facility: The method and timing are shaped by the facility rather than a universal promise.
