How service starts

We learn the building before we build the plan.

Premier's process starts with conversation, not a generic package. The cleaning scope comes from how the facility works and what the client needs handled well.

  1. Talk about the facility

    Discuss the current arrangement, frustrations, schedule, expectations, and priorities.

  2. Walk the space

    Donald personally walks the facility to understand how it works, not just to measure it.

  3. Define the scope

    The plan is shaped around layout, traffic, operating hours, priority areas, and preferences.

  4. Start with expectations

    A consistent crew starts the work with clear expectations for what the service covers.

  5. Stay accountable

    After service begins, Donald keeps visiting and inspecting, and changing needs go directly to him.
