How service starts

We learn the building before we build the plan.

Premier's process starts with conversation, not a generic package. The cleaning scope comes from how the facility operates and what the client needs handled well.

  1. Talk about the facility

    Discuss the current arrangement, frustrations, schedule, expectations, and priorities.

  2. Walk the space

    Donald personally walks the facility to understand how it operates, not just to measure it.

  3. Define the scope

    The plan is shaped around layout, traffic, operating hours, priority areas, and preferences.

  4. Start with expectations

    Premier assigns the work and establishes what the service is expected to cover.

  5. Stay accountable

    After service begins, clients can discuss changing needs directly with the responsible person.
